The service

TextBack Desk sets up and manages an automatic text message that is sent when a business misses a phone call. It is text-only. It does not answer or place phone calls, and it does not dispatch technicians or book jobs by itself.

Pricing and billing

Pricing is confirmed in writing before you pay anything. The current offer is a founding price for the first 5 clients ($99 setup and $127 per month), with standard pricing of $299 setup and $197 per month afterwards. The service is month-to-month and you can cancel anytime.

No guarantees

I do not guarantee a number of leads, jobs or revenue. Results vary by business and call volume. Example conversations and sample dashboards on this website are illustrations, not real customer results.

Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate business information.
  • In the US, complete the A2P 10DLC carrier registration, which can take days to a couple of weeks. Texts may not go live on day one.
  • Use the service in line with applicable messaging and consumer-protection rules, including honoring opt-outs.
