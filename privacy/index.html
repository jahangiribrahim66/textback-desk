Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This is a plain-language starter policy written for a small, email-only service. It is not legal advice. Have it reviewed against your actual setup before relying on it.

Who I am

TextBack Desk is run by Jahangir. You can reach me at textbackdesk.jahangir@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • If you email me: your name, email address and whatever you choose to send.
  • If you become a client: business details needed for setup, such as your business name, address, website, the phone number customers call, and the services you offer.
  • For missed-call texts: the caller’s phone number and the text messages exchanged, so the conversation can reach you.

How I use it

I use this information only to run the service, reply to you, and meet carrier and legal requirements. I do not sell personal information.

Text messages and opt-out

Every automatic text includes an opt-out line. A person who replies STOP should not receive further messages.

This website

This site does not use forms, accounts or advertising trackers. It uses your device's standard fonts and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. Contacting me by email is the only way to share information through it.

Where data is handled

I work from the United Arab Emirates. Missed-call texts are sent and stored using third-party messaging and phone platforms that I choose for each client, so some data is processed in the United States or other countries. I will tell you which platform is used before setup.

Your choices

You can ask me to access, correct or delete the information I hold about you by emailing textbackdesk.jahangir@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
