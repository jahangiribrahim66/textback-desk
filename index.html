Questions

What is missed-call text-back?

It is an automatic text sent to a customer when their call to your business goes unanswered. It keeps the conversation open so the customer does not call the next company.

Is this an AI that answers my phone calls?

No. This service is text-only. It does not answer or place phone calls. It sends a text after a missed call and collects the customer's job details by text.

Do I need to register my business to send these texts in the US?

Yes. US carriers require A2P 10DLC registration for business texts sent from a standard local number, including automatic missed-call replies. It needs your legal business name, address, website and usually an EIN. Approval can take days to a couple of weeks, so texts may not go live on day one.
