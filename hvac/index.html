FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does TextBack Desk work?

When a customer calls your business number and nobody answers, an automatic text goes out to that caller. The customer can reply, the conversation collects their job details, and you see the lead so you can call back the ones worth your time. It is text-only: it does not answer or place phone calls.

Can the text tell emergency calls from routine ones?

The text asks one simple question about what is wrong. You read the reply and decide who to call back first. It does not dispatch a technician or book a job by itself.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. You keep your existing business number and your customers keep calling it. The exact call-handling setup depends on your phone provider and is worked out with you during setup.

How quickly does the customer receive a text?

The goal is within about 30 seconds of the missed call. In the US, business texting first needs A2P 10DLC carrier registration (your legal business name, address, website and usually an EIN). Approval can take days to a couple of weeks, so texts may not go live on day one.

Is there a setup fee?

Yes. The founding price for the first 5 clients is $99 setup and $127 per month. Standard pricing after that is $299 setup and $197 per month. The exact price is confirmed in writing before you pay anything.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. It is month-to-month and you can cancel anytime.
